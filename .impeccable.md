# Design Context

## Users
Prospective ecommerce store owners viewing a sales demo (in a meeting or on their phone), judging what their own storefront could look like.

## Brand personality
Editorial, warm, tactile, confident. Fictional fashion brand "Halden": considered clothing in wool, linen and leather.

## Aesthetic direction
Bone paper background, warm ink text, one rust accent. Fraunces (display serif) with Hanken Grotesk (body). Arch-shaped product frames, asymmetric layouts, generous whitespace. Avoid generic AI aesthetics: no purple gradients, no icon-card grids, no dark-glow looks.

## Principles
- The page must prove the platform: every interactive element really works.
- Mobile is a first-class context, not a shrink-down.
- Motion is purposeful (entrances, cart drawer) and respects reduced-motion.
